# EQL recipe data attribution

`eql-recipes.json` is a normalized snapshot of wiki-derived structured recipe records exposed by the EQL Helper public catalog.

- Source catalog: https://eql.gamertan.com/developers/api
- Source and rights statement: https://eql.gamertan.com/sources
- Original community source: EverQuest Legends Wiki contributors, with individual page and revision links retained in each record
- License for the adapted wiki-derived structured material: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)

EQL Helper's software and original site presentation are not copied or licensed here. EverQuest, EverQuest Legends, game artwork, icons, maps, and related marks remain the property of their respective rights holders. EQLSaK does not bundle recipe icons from the game.

Recipe availability, trivial values, containers, and components can change. EQLSaK keeps the source evidence visible and does not promote legacy/unverified records to EQL-confirmed status.
