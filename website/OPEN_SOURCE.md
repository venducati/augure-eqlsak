# Open-source and content policy

## What is open source

All original application source code in this repository is licensed under MIT. This includes the interface, search and routing logic, local log reader, character profile tools, provenance system, and 3D viewer code.

The project uses open-source dependencies. Their own license terms and copyright notices continue to apply.

## What is not automatically covered

The MIT software license does not grant rights to EverQuest or EverQuest Legends names, trademarks, game client files, or third-party maps, images, models, text, and datasets. Contributors must not submit extracted proprietary game assets or material they do not have permission to redistribute.

Original EQLSaK artwork may be redistributed only when its file or accompanying attribution record explicitly states a license. Unmarked art and data are not automatically relicensed by the repository's MIT license.

## Safe contribution rules

- Submit original code and assets, or material with documented redistribution permission.
- Record the source and verification status of factual game data.
- Keep `User-Verified EQL`, `EQL-specific source`, and `Legacy EQ unverified` labels intact.
- Never convert an approximate NPC coordinate into an exact claim without evidence.
- Do not include player logs, account information, credentials, or other personal data.
- Preserve the unofficial-project notice and avoid implying endorsement by a rights holder.

## Community distribution

Forks and modified builds should retain the MIT copyright and permission notice, identify their changes, and preserve accurate provenance labels. A public EQLSaK release should pass the documented release gates before being presented as community-ready.
